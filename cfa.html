CFA Level I · your 2027 attempt

Build your study plan

Pick a window, your start date and the hours you can sustain. Dates default to the first day of the target month as a conservative planning placeholder; replace it with your confirmed appointment.

    Budget is adjustable, not a pass guarantee. Keep 2–3 hours weekly for applied finance until your final six weeks. Official scheduling and retake policy: attempts are limited and consecutive-window retakes are not permitted; confirm eligibility before choosing another window.

    Use the 2027 syllabus

    Level I normally runs in February, May, August and November. Choose a 2027 window, check the official calendar, then enter your booked date in the planner above. Month selections are planning targets, not confirmed exam dates or registration deadlines. Registration and appointment scheduling are separate steps.

    Start with the official Level I outline and the Learning Ecosystem after registration. The 2027 update changes the curriculum. Old topic weights and old notes need checking.

    Complete one Practical Skills Module to receive your result. Choose a finance / analyst-oriented module if offered rather than repeating skills you already have; availability must be checked at registration.

    A rhythm that travels across exam windows

    Use roughly 55% of your available weeks for the first syllabus pass, 25% for mixed practice and weak-topic repair, and 20% for timed mocks and revision. For a new finance learner, a short runway may demand more hours than a full-time job allows; choose a later window rather than skipping topics.

    Study accounting and discounting first, then corporate finance, equities and fixed income. Cover economics, derivatives, alternatives, portfolio construction and quantitative gaps next. Revisit ethics every week. Reserve the final six weeks for mocks and error-log repair; complete the PSM by the official deadline.

    Four short weekday sessions plus two focused weekend blocks usually fit a job better than one marathon. Keep one recovery evening. The best plan is the one you can repeat.

    Readiness and a small resource stack

    Use CFA’s curriculum, questions and mocks as the spine. Add Kaplan Schweser or Mark Meldrum only if you need a different explanation; choose one, not a subscription collection. Confirm the provider supports the 2027 syllabus before purchase.

    My readiness heuristic: last three timed mocks average at least 70%, no repeated conceptual gaps, and stable pacing. This is not CFA’s minimum passing score. Check current exam format, permitted calculators and exam-day rules on the official exam page.

    Budget separately for registration, taxes / currency conversion, calculator, optional provider, travel and a contingency. Prices change: use checkout and official scholarships, rather than a fixed INR estimate.
