Choose by the work, not the bank logo

Score an offer on decision exposure (30%), finance depth (25%), manager / mentoring (20%), analytical ownership (15%) and compensation (10%). Prefer a role where you present a recommendation and see the outcome. Ask: “What decision did the last analyst influence? How often do I meet finance leaders? Who owns the forecast or risk policy?”

Before accepting, compare fixed salary, variable pay, stock vesting, joining bonus clawback, hours, shift timing and mobility. Generic data engineering can preserve salary, but needs a deliberate move toward financial ownership within 12–18 months.
